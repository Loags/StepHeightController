# Publisher media

Approved text-free artwork is stored as editable SVGs and upload-ready PNGs. The final Rigidbody demo screenshots and video are in `demos-v5/`.

Rebuild artwork with `npm install` and `npm run build` in this folder. The four PNGs match the publisher slots: icon 160×160, card 420×280, cover 1950×1300 and social 1200×630. See [BRANDING.md](BRANDING.md) for framing guidance.

The five screenshots show stepping, its disabled comparison, jumping, first-person movement and blocked clearance. The 23.5-second video uses real Unity gameplay; its wider third-person capture framing was adjusted only for composition. Starter Assets visuals retain the license and notices included in the Unity package.

Archives, upload automation and validation records remain local under the Unity project's ignored `ReleaseArtifacts/` folder. Portal screenshots, rejected media, QA images and intermediate captures are also ignored. The personal publishing skill is installed outside this repository.
